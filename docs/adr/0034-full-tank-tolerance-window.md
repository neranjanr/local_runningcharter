# Full-Tank Tolerance Window [cap-3, cap+1] with floating post-pump to minimize variation

Strict post-pump was `[cap-3, cap]` with the balance forced to `cap`, so a Full Tank that would force an 8→6 km/L jump had no relief. Grill 2026-10-03 decided fuel measurement is imprecise to ~1 L: a Full-Tank post-pump is feasible in `[cap-3, cap+1]` (72–76 L for 75 L), may sit at `cap-2`/`cap-3` (Full−2) when that avoids a step (8→6 becomes 7.5→6.8 by assuming 72 L), floats inside the window to minimize `|economy − prev|` in both Normal and Strict passes (Normal still step-capped, Strict uncapped), carries forward as the next segment's opening, and validates/ledgers identically (`validateFuelFeasibility`, `calculateBalance` Full path caps at `cap+1`). Only a Full-Tank post-pump may exceed `cap`; all other intermediates stay ≤ `cap`.
