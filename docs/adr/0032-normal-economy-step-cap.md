# Normal economy step cap vs feasibility-first Strict

Normal estimation used to always snap to the nearest feasible economy, so when feasibility forced a jump (a segment that would run the tank dry or overflow before its next fill) it produced large steps such as 9.7 → 5.3 km/L, which read as arbitrary even though they were the only feasible values. Grill 2026-09-28 decided Normal should cap each step from the previous economy at 1.5 km/L and flag the segment infeasible with a warning when the cap bites, while the Strict Full-Tank column stays feasibility-first (it targets a full post-pump balance and may jump as far as the data requires). The two columns then give the operator a smooth option and a physical option.

This is a deliberate trade-off: a capped Normal suggestion can leave the modelled balance outside `[1, tankCapacity]`, which is why the row is flagged. The alternative (feasibility-first Normal) was rejected because the resulting large steps mislead the operator into thinking the estimator is broken when the recorded distances and fills are simply inconsistent with the tank size.

Consequences: `runEstimationPass` takes a `MAX_STEP = 1.5` cap on the lenient path only; capped segments report `feasible: false` with a "Step capped" warning; the displayed `prevEconomy` chain is recomputed after smoothing so the `Prev` column matches the final suggestions; the estimator seeds from the earliest applied (unlocked) economy so re-runs stay anchored to the book while still re-estimating every unlocked segment. `CONTEXT.md` updates `Estimated Fuel Economy`.

## Amendment: tiered cap for long-trip segments (2026-09-28)

The flat 1.5 km/L cap under-reached on segments made of long runs: a segment whose feasible floor sat 2–3 km/L above the previous economy was forced to a capped value that left the balance outside `[1, tankCapacity]`, even though long runs genuinely give better economy. `stepCapFor` now widens the Normal cap by the same `>40 km` / `≥100 km` tiers already shown as distance badges: base 1.5 km/L, 2.5 km/L when the segment contains any Trip over 40 km, 3.0 km/L when it contains any Trip 100 km or longer (highest tier wins). Segments using a widened cap are skipped by the lenient smoothing pass so the deliberate step is not pulled back toward its neighbours. The capped warning names the tier (e.g. "Step capped at 2.5 km/L from 7.8 (nearest feasible 10.6, >40 km trips)"). Strict remains feasibility-first and uncapped, and segments with no long trips keep the historic 1.5 km/L behaviour.
