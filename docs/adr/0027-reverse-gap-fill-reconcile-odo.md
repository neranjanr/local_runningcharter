# ADR 0027: Reverse Gap Fill to Reconcile Final ODO

Decided: add global `Reverse Gap Fill` (small button inside `Continuity Alert`) that asks for Physical ODO or reverse-shift Δ (`Δ = recordedFinal − physicalOdo`, Integer KM, synced reciprocals) and chronologically compresses positive KM Gaps earliest-first (`G1→max(0,G1−Δ)`, spill to `G2…`) shifting each tail segment backward by its consumed share (`trip_distance` frozen, `BookPage` + fuel chain recomputed from `Book Opening` via `recalculatePageBalancesFromOpening`), rejected when `Δ≤0` (physical > recorded → "add missing trips") or `Δ>totalPositiveGapExtent` (hint `max compressible = recordedFinal−totalGaps` + one-click fill), disabled when overlaps `extent<0` and hidden when no KM gaps; dialog carries a `⚠️ Fuel Position & Closing Balances will be recalculated` warning and a per-gap `before→after` preview before atomic `fleetledger:data-changed` commit.

Context: operators reported ledgers reaching e.g. 10000 km with ~50 km total vacuums (e.g. Jan `0→800`, Feb `900→1000` gap 100 but physical final `920` → Δ80 shrinks gap to 20). Prior gap tooling only handled exact `Gap Fill Trip` (no shift) and forward `Gap-Absorbed Insert` (bounded forward shift `lib/tripShift.ts:94`); no backward reconciliation existed, and a uniform backward shift would overlap the first gap when `Δ>G1`. We chose earliest-first absorption because it mirrors the forward bounded logic, guarantees `G'≥0` with no overlap, and matches the intuition that oldest missing km is compressed first, over proportional or latest-first alternatives. Dual ODO/Δ input covers operators who know either the physical reading or the delta, and the strict reject-instead-of-clamp avoids silently leaving the physical ODO unreconciled.

Consequences: new pure helper `shiftForReverseGapFill(sortedTrips, physicalOdo)` in `lib/tripShift.ts` (sequential earliest-first), preview modal reuses `Insert After` patterns, and `Vehicle.current_odometer` is optionally updated to the entered physical ODO (checkbox, default on) so the `Digital Vehicle Cluster` visor matches the reconciled book.
